What does the per-person price include?

Your stay in the private 5BHK villa, the swimming pool, and four meals: breakfast, lunch, high tea and dinner. There is no separate charge for food.

Why is the weekend rate for 10+ guests only?

The villa has 5 bedrooms and is priced for groups. For smaller groups on weekends, send us your dates and guest count and we will share the best rate.

Does the rate change in December?

Until 14 December: ₹3,500 per person on weekends (10+ guests) and ₹3,000 on weekdays. From 15 December, the rate is ₹4,000 per person for every night, with the same villa, pool and meals.
